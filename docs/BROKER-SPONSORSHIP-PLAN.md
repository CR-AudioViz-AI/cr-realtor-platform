## The problem this solves

Zoyzy has nationwide listing data — thirteen MLSs confirmed live through RentCast
at $0 — but **no photographs**. Verified across every RentCast endpoint: no media
fields exist. Roy's judgement, and it is correct: *"I would not put my listings
on a service that can't show the photos to the customers."*

Photos live in the RESO **Media** resource. That resource reaches only three
pipes — MLS Grid, Trestle, Bridge — and all three require MLS participatory
rights.

MLS Grid's own FAQ says data is *"made accessible to brokers **and vendors**
through ONE data feed."* Their vendor page lists iHomefinder, IDX Broker,
InCom, Inside Real Estate, HomeSpotter, Lone Wolf — technology companies, not
brokerages.

**One broker sponsoring us as their IDX vendor unlocks the entire MLS**, with
photography, because an IDX feed carries every participating broker's listings —
not only the sponsor's.

---

## STOP — the structure Roy proposed will not work as stated

The proposal was: split lead income 70/30 in exchange for sponsorship.

**Do not offer a commission split.** Two rules make it dangerous:

**State licence law.** In Florida and most states, receiving compensation for
real estate services — including referring a buyer to an agent — requires a real
estate licence. CR AudioViz AI holds none.

**RESPA Section 8.** Federal law bans giving or accepting a fee for the referral
of settlement-service business. A per-lead or per-closing split is the textbook
example.

This is not a technicality. It is the difference between a business and an
enforcement action, and it would surface at exactly the moment Zoyzy became
visible enough to matter.

**How Zillow avoids both:** they sell **advertising**, never a referral fee and
never a share of commission. An agent buys placement. What happens after is
between the agent and the client. That is why Zillow operates in fifty states
without a brokerage licence — and it is worth noting Zillow's own 35–40%
"referral fee" applies only through Zillow Flex, where they *are* licensed.

Our own pricing page already attacks that fee. We cannot copy the thing we are
attacking.

## The offer, restructured

Everything Roy wants, none of the exposure. Nothing here is a share of
commission and nothing is contingent on a transaction closing.

### What the founding broker gives

1. Sponsors CR AudioViz AI as their IDX technology vendor with their MLS
2. Signs the MLS Grid (or equivalent) vendor licence alongside us
3. Fifteen minutes of feedback a month during the first six months

### What the founding broker gets

1. **Free forever.** Never billed for the platform, at any tier, for as long as
   the sponsorship stands. Written into the agreement, not a promotion.
2. **Exclusive founding placement in their market** — their brokerage is the
   named partner on every consumer page in that MLS's territory.
3. **Every lead from their own listings, free, in perpetuity.** Not a share.
   Not a split. A consumer enquiring about their listing reaches them and only
   them, and we are never paid for it.
4. **Their agents on the platform free**, including the AI tools other
   brokerages will pay $249/month for.
5. **Full data export, always.** 100% data ownership is already our published
   promise; this makes it contractual for the sponsor.
6. **Recruitment advantage.** A brokerage that offers agents a Zillow
   alternative with no lead resale is a brokerage agents move to. That is worth
   more to a broker than any revenue share.

### What CR AudioViz AI gets

1. IDX feed with Media for that MLS — every participating broker's listings,
   with photographs
2. The right to sell subscriptions to **other** brokerages and agents in that
   market
3. A named brokerage partner, which makes the second and third MLS
   conversations dramatically easier

**The sponsor's own business is never monetised. Revenue comes from everyone
else.** That is what keeps this an advertising business rather than a
referral-fee business.

The pitch in one line: *Zillow takes your listing, generates a lead from it,
and sells that lead back to you for 35–40% of your commission. We will show your
listing, send you the lead, and charge you nothing — ever.*

A broker who has watched that happen for a decade will take the meeting.

The ask is genuinely small. Sponsoring an IDX vendor is routine — every
brokerage with a website has already done it for iHomefinder, IDX Broker or
similar. We are asking to be added, not to replace anything.

## Honest risks

**The MLS may refuse a vendor with no existing broker clients.** MLS Grid's FAQ
implies sponsorship is required but does not say a vendor cannot be new. This is
the single question to resolve before anything else.

**Exclusivity may conflict with MLS display rules.** IDX agreements carry rules
about how listings are shown and whether a broker can be given preferential
display. Legal review, per MLS.

**A sponsor may want equity or exclusivity we cannot give.** Decide the ceiling
before the first call, not during it.

**Traffic is the real gate.** Free access to a market nobody visits is worth
nothing, and a founding broker who feels sold to will say so publicly in a
market where everyone knows everyone.
